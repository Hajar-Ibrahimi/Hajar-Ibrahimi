## 👋 About Me

I’m a CBBDA Master's student interested in applying data science and artificial intelligence to biomedical and healthcare problems.

My work spans EEG signal processing, medical imaging, bioinformatics, statistical analysis, machine learning, and deep learning — with a focus on building careful, interpretable, and reproducible analytical workflows.

## 🔬 What I've Built

### 🧠 EEG-Based Emotion Classification — SEED

Subject-independent EEG emotion recognition using Differential Entropy features, topographic mapping, CNN, LSTM, BiLSTM, and CNN–LSTM Fusion architectures with Leave-One-Subject-Out evaluation.

### 🧠 Epileptic Seizure Recognition — Feature Engineering & Machine Learning

A leakage-aware EEG classification pipeline combining time-domain, frequency-domain, wavelet, and nonlinear features with feature selection and group-aware model evaluation.

### 🧠 EEG Seizure Recognition — 2001–2025

A methodological exploration of seizure recognition approaches spanning nonlinear analysis, wavelets, CNNs, ensemble learning, and hybrid CNN-feature/XGBoost modeling.

### 🧬 Louvain vs. Leiden — PBMC3k

Comparison of graph-based community detection methods on single-cell RNA-seq data using KNN graphs, UMAP, resolution analysis, ARI, and NMI.

### 🔬 DBSCAN vs. HDBSCAN — Diagnostic Data

Comparison of density-based approaches for identifying low-density observations in the Breast Cancer Wisconsin dataset, including parameter sensitivity and feature-level profiling.

### 🔬 Outlier Detection — KNN vs. Mahalanobis

Comparison of KNN and Average-KNN outlier scoring under Euclidean, Manhattan, and Mahalanobis distances, with consensus and sensitivity analysis.

### 🔬 Multivariate Outlier Detection — Hotelling’s T²

Statistical analysis of atypical diagnostic profiles using multivariate standardization, covariance modeling, Hotelling’s T², and chi-square thresholding.

### 🩻 Brain Tumor MRI — Data Quality & Preprocessing

Medical image inspection and preprocessing focused on data integrity, duplicate detection, train–test leakage identification, augmentation, and standardized TensorFlow/PyTorch pipelines.

### 🎬 Netflix Catalog Analysis

Exploratory data analysis of the Netflix catalog combining data cleaning, transformation, visualization, IMDb enrichment, rating analysis, and pairwise cast analysis.
